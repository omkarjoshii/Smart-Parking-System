How it works

1. Book
Enter your details, vehicle number, arrival and exit time and choose a free slot.
2. Pay
Pay by UPI or card, or choose to pay at the exit.
3. Park
The entrance sensor opens the gate. The slot sensor confirms your arrival.
4. Exit
On exit the admin closes your booking and the final amount is calculated.
